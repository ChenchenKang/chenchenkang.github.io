Amiga Flex Arrives at Our lab

The Amiga Flex, the newest version of the Amiga robot, has arrived at our lab. It will serve as a R&D platform for developing robotic solutions for nursery crop production, including autonomous spraying, robotic weeding, and granular herbicide spreading.

During the meeting, he reconnected with former colleagues, met new researchers and industry professionals, explored opportunities for future collaborations, and learned about emerging research and technologies in agricultural and biological engineering. July 08, 2026

Kang Receives WPI–TSU Collaborative Seed Grant

Kang, together with Dr. Bo Tang and Dr. Xinming Huang of Worcester Polytechnic Institute, received a WPI–TSU Collaborative Seed Grant for the project “A Collaborative Drone-Ground Robotic System for Inventory Mapping in Field-Grown Nursery Production.” The project will integrate aerial drones, ground robots, cameras, and LiDAR sensors to develop an automated inventory-mapping system for field-grown nursery production.

Kang Presents on Robotic Spraying and Hosts Lab Tour at NRC Field Day

Kang delivered an Extension presentation titled “Robotic Spraying in Specialty Crops—Progress Today, Potential for Nurseries Tomorrow” at the 2026 Otis L. Floyd Nursery Research Center Field Day in McMinnville, Tennessee. The presentation highlighted recent advances in robotic spraying and their potential applications in nursery production. Kang Attends FIRA USA 2025

Kang attended FIRA USA 2025 in Woodland, California, from October 21 to 23. October 15, 2025

Kang Visits Hale & Hines Nursery

Kang visited Hale & Hines Nursery in McMinnville, Tennessee, and met with nursery president Terry Hines, who provided a tour of the nursery. They had a productive discussion about automation, precision ag technologies, and opportunities to connect research and extension programs with the practical needs of nursery growers. Since helping establish Hale & Hines Nursery in 1978, Hines has devoted his career to advancing nursery production through innovations in pot-in-pot production, precision irrigation, equipment improvement, and operational efficiency. His lifelong commitment to practical innovation and sharing knowledge has made a lasting contribution to the nursery industry.

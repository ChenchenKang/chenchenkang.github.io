summary: >
  Kang visited Hale & Hines Nursery in McMinnville, Tennessee, and met
  with nursery president Terry Hines, who provided a tour of the nursery.
  They had a productive discussion about automation, precision ag
  technologies, and opportunities to connect research and extension
  programs with the practical needs of nursery growers. Since helping
  establish Hale & Hines Nursery in 1978, Hines has devoted his career
  to advancing nursery production through innovations in pot-in-pot
  production, precision irrigation, equipment improvement, and operational efficiency. His lifelong
  commitment to practical innovation and sharing knowledge has made
  a lasting contribution to the nursery industry.

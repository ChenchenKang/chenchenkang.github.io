  Kang delivered an Extension presentation titled “Robotic Spraying in
  Specialty Crops—Progress Today, Potential for Nurseries Tomorrow” at the
  2026 Otis L. Floyd Nursery Research Center Field Day in McMinnville,
  Tennessee. The presentation highlighted recent advances in robotic spraying
  and their potential applications in nursery production. Following the
  presentation, Kang hosted a laboratory tour to introduce ongoing research,
  sensing and robotic platforms, and future directions for automation in
  specialty crop production.

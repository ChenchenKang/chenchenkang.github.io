summary: >
  Kang attended FIRA USA 2025 in Woodland, California, from October 21
  to 23. Through live field demonstrations, exhibitions, and technical
  discussions, he explored the latest agricultural robots and autonomous
  technologies for specialty crop production. The event also provided
  opportunities to connect with growers, researchers, robotics companies,
  and technology developers, exchange ideas about the practical adoption
  of agricultural automation, and explore potential collaborations related
  to robotic spraying, weeding, material application, and other solutions
  for specialty crop production. A photograph of Kang viewing robotic
  technologies at the event was later featured by FIRA in promotional
  material for FIRA USA 2026, which will be held October 20–22, 2026,
  in Yakima, Washington.

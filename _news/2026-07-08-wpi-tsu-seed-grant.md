inline: true

summary: >
  Kang, together with Dr. Bo Tang and Dr. Xinming Huang of Worcester
  Polytechnic Institute, received a WPI–TSU Collaborative Seed Grant
  for the project “A Collaborative Drone-Ground Robotic System for
  Inventory Mapping in Field-Grown Nursery Production.” The project
  will integrate aerial drones, ground robots, cameras, and LiDAR
  sensors to develop an automated inventory-mapping system for
  field-grown nursery production.

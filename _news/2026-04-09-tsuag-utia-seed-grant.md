  Kang and Dr. Yanqiu Yang of the University of Tennessee Institute of
  Agriculture received a $50,000 TSUAg–UTIA Collaborative Seed Grant for
  the project “A Low-Cost Microclimate Monitoring Platform for Smart
  Agriculture with Case Studies in Nursery and Beef Production.” The
  project will develop an affordable monitoring platform to support
  data-driven management and decision-making in agricultural production.
